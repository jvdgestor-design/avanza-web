Privacy notice

Controller: Jose Vicente Díaz Madrid (Grupo Avanza Consultores), C/ Joan Baptista Benlloch, 15, 46980 Paterna (Valencia), Spain; avanza@grupoavanzaconsultores.es.

Purpose

We answer enquiries received by WhatsApp or email and, where applicable, manage the business relationship that results from them.

Legal bases

The legal bases are the performance of a contract or of steps taken at the request of the data subject before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679) and, for retention during the periods set by tax and commercial law, compliance with a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)).

Retention

The data are kept for as long as the business relationship lasts and, afterwards, for the limitation periods under tax and commercial law that apply in each case.

Recipients

Data are disclosed only where the law requires it or where it is necessary for the service you have engaged, to the Spanish Tax Agency, the Social Security, public registries or notaries. For the contact form, the information below on technical providers also applies.

Your rights

You may exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection at the address given above. You may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish data protection authority, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD).

Contact form

The form on the contact page is for requesting information about our services. The following applies to the data sent through it.

Data: your name, your email address or WhatsApp number, a description of your case and, if you give it, how you found us. The page you write from is also sent. Please do not include health data or documents belonging to third parties in your enquiry. If we receive such information, it is deleted.

Purpose and legal basis: to answer your enquiry and, if you so wish, to prepare the service. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, under Article 6(1)(b) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679. If you tell us how you found us, we also use that information to learn through which channels enquiries reach us, without building profiles; the legal basis is our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)), and you may object at any time by email. These data are not used for advertising.

Transfer: the form sends your enquiry through Google Apps Script, a Google service, to the email inboxes of our practice. These are provided by Google (Gmail) and by the email provider of our domain. Subject to legal obligations, these are the only recipients. Google LLC may process the data in the United States. These transfers rely on Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/1795 on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. Google LLC is listed there as an active participant (official list of participants, checked on 3 October 2026).

Retention of enquiries: while your enquiry is being dealt with. If no engagement follows, the data are deleted from the inboxes and from Apps Script once the enquiry is closed. If you engage us, the periods described above apply.

Rights: as described above; please write to avanza@grupoavanzaconsultores.es.

Required information: your name, your email address or WhatsApp number and a description of your case are needed to reply to you. Telling us how you found us is optional. There is no automated decision-making and no profiling.

Modelo 210 calculator for non-resident property owners in Spain

By Grupo Avanza Consultores

An indicative calculation of the Spanish Non-Resident Income Tax (Modelo 210, also called Form 210) due from an owner with a property in Spain, in two cases: a home kept at the owner’s disposal and not let (imputed income), and a home that is let. It runs in your browser: no data is sent, nothing is stored and it uses no cookies.

Indicative result. This calculator does not replace the tax return or a review of your case. It does not take into account double taxation treaties, usufruct and other rights in rem, several periods, other income of the same taxpayer, properties under construction or that cannot be used for planning reasons, or surcharges, interest or penalties. The figures you enter are the ones that count: if any of them is wrong, so is the result.

This page only calculates; it does not file the return. How filing works: Modelo 210 for non-residents.

Rules applied

Which articles the calculator uses

Consolidated text of the Spanish Official Gazette (BOE), read on 16 November 2026. The consolidated text is for information: what counts is the official publication. The legal texts are in Spanish.

  • Article 24.1 of the consolidated text of the Non-Resident Income Tax Law (Royal Legislative Decree 5/2004). Tax base: the gross amount, with no multiplying percentages or reductions. Updated on 28 November 2014. See in the BOE.
  • Article 24.5 of the same text. The imputed income from properties of non-resident individuals is determined under Article 87 of the 2004 consolidated text of the Personal Income Tax Law, repealed by Law 35/2006. The calculator applies Article 85 of Law 35/2006 (imputation of property income). This is also the criterion published by the Spanish Tax Agency for non-residents, which determines the imputed income «con arreglo a la normativa del IRPF» (in accordance with the personal income tax rules) (sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es, Renta imputada de inmueble urbano para uso propio, consulted on 16 November 2026). See in the BOE.
  • Article 24.6 of the same text. Residents of another Member State of the European Union, and of the European Economic Area with an effective exchange of information: they may deduct the expenses provided for in Law 35/2006 if they prove that these are directly related to the income obtained in Spain and have a direct and inseparable economic link with the activity carried out in Spain. See in the BOE.
  • Article 25.1.a of the same text. Rate of 24%; 19% for residents of another Member State of the European Union or of the European Economic Area with an effective exchange of tax information. Updated on 28 November 2014. For Liechtenstein, the 19% rate and the deduction of expenses apply from 11 July 2021 (criterion of the Spanish Tax Agency; additional provision ten, paragraph 3, of Law 36/2006, added by Law 11/2021); before that, 24% applies with no expenses. See in the BOE.
  • Article 85 of Law 35/2006 on Personal Income Tax. Imputed income: 2% of the cadastral value, in proportion to the number of days; 1.1% if the cadastral values of the municipality have been revised, modified or determined by a general collective valuation and came into force in the tax period or in the ten preceding ones; 1.1% of 50% of the higher of the checked value and the acquisition value if the property has no cadastral value or it has not been notified. Wording in force on 16 November 2026 (block updated on 7 October 2026). The scale approved by Royal Decree-law 29/2026 (ratified on 12 November 2026) takes effect from 1 January 2027 and is not applied. See in the BOE.
  • Additional provision fifty-five of Law 35/2006. In the 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026 tax periods, 1.1% applies if the cadastral values revised, modified or determined by a general collective valuation came into force on or after 1 January 2012. Wording of Royal Decree-law 29/2026 (ratified on 12 November 2026, BOE of 13 November 2026), with effect from 1 January 2026; for 2023 it is the wording of Law 31/2022, and for 2024 and 2025 the same criterion is the one applied in the Spanish Tax Agency’s manuals. For 2016 to 2022 the calculator applies the criterion of Article 85.1 (in force in the tax period or in the ten preceding ones). See in the BOE.
  • Article 11.3 of Law 35/2006. Attribution of income to the owners of the assets (hence the ownership percentage). See in the BOE.
  • Article 23.1 of Law 35/2006. Deductible expenses for income from immovable property, to which Article 24.6 refers; used only for the note on the expenses field. See in the BOE.
  • Article 5 of Order EHA/3316/2010. The wording of Order HAC/623/2026 (in force since 24 June 2026) applies to income accrued from 2026; earlier years follow the wording in force at the time. Imputed income (letter b): accruals up to 2025, the calendar year following accrual (1 January to 31 December); 2026 accruals, 1 April to 31 December of the following year. Rental income with tax to pay (letter c, 1): 2016 to 2023 accruals, quarterly (the first twenty days of April, July, October and January); 2024 and 2025, 1 to 20 January of the following year if the year is grouped, or quarterly if reported separately (wording of Order HAC/56/2024); 2026, 1 to 20 April of the following year if the income is grouped or the fourth quarter is reported separately, with the quarterly deadline for the first to third quarters of 2026 reported separately. Zero tax (letter c, 2): 1 to 20 January of the following year. See in the BOE.

How amounts are rounded

Amounts are calculated in cents and rounded to the cent, half a cent upwards, in three steps: income from the property, the owner’s tax base and the tax.

What it does not calculate

It does not calculate depreciation or expenses: you enter the expenses figure. It does not apply the reductions for letting a home in Article 23.2 of Law 35/2006, because Article 24.1 excludes reductions. It does not calculate capital gains on a sale, pensions, withholdings or the tax under a treaty. Nor does it calculate the rental income of a Liechtenstein resident in 2021 (the rate and the expenses change on 11 July 2021 and the calculator does not split income by date): in that case it gives no result. Nor does it decide whether a home is your main residence or whether the property is used in a business activity.

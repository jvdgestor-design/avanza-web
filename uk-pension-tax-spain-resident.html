UK pension in Spain: State Pension, tax returns and HMRC relief

Esta guía en español →

If you live in Spain, are tax resident here under the ordinary income tax rules and are resident in Spain for the purposes of the Spain–UK tax treaty, your UK State Pension is taxed only in Spain under the tax treaty. A UK government service pension follows a different treaty rule. Grupo Avanza Consultores in Paterna (Valencia) checks your pension type and residence, prepares your Spanish tax return when required and helps request the AEAT certificate and HMRC relief form. We advise remotely across Spain, in written English by email, with a quote before we start.

The general rule: it is taxed in Spain

Pensions and other similar remuneration paid to an individual who is a resident of a State may be taxed only in that State, except for the public pensions covered by article 18.2 (article 17 of the Spain–United Kingdom double taxation convention, in force since 12 June 2014). Treaty residence is determined under article 4, including its tie-break rules where both countries regard you as resident. Under that general rule, if you are resident in Spain for the purposes of the treaty, your UK pension is taxed here and not there. The UK itself lists the State Pension, occupational pensions and purchased annuities on the form a Spanish resident uses to ask for UK tax not to be deducted.

The exception: public service pensions

Pensions paid by the UK State, its political subdivisions or local authorities, either directly or out of funds created by them, for services rendered to them are taxable only in the UK, unless the pensioner is both resident in Spain for the purposes of the treaty and a Spanish national, in which case they are taxable only in Spain (article 18.2 of the convention). Someone who also holds Spanish nationality is a Spanish national for this purpose (article 3.1.j). Those paid for services rendered in connection with a business carried on by the State, its political subdivisions or local authorities follow the general rule (article 18.3). Where a government pension is taxed only in the UK, Spain may take it into account when calculating the tax on the rest of your income (article 22.1.b).

How it is declared in Spain

Pensions from public social security schemes and other public retirement benefits are declared as employment income (article 17.2.a of the Spanish Personal Income Tax Act). For private pensions, the classification depends on the type of scheme and is checked against its documents; annuities purchased from an insurer, for example, are generally investment income, unless they must be taxed as employment income (articles 17.2.a and 25.3). For annual gross employment income, the general 22,000 euro filing threshold falls to 15,876 euros when the payer is not required to withhold Spanish income tax (article 96.2.a and 3.c). These are the rules in force on 5 November 2026; the relevant tax year and any other filing obligations must be checked. A pension exempt in Spain is not taken into account for the duty to file, according to the Spanish Tax Agency, and anyone entitled to the international double taxation deduction must file if they claim it (article 96.4 of the Act and article 61.1 of its Regulation). That deduction only covers what the UK may tax under the convention (article 80 of the Act and article 22.1.a of the convention); if UK tax was deducted from a pension the convention assigns to Spain, it is not credited in the Spanish return: it is reclaimed from HMRC.

If the UK keeps deducting tax

Where the treaty assigns the pension to Spain, apply to HMRC for relief or repayment using form “DT-Individual Spain” (version HMRC 10/22). Attach the original AEAT certificate for treaty purposes, “Residencia Fiscal en España Convenio”; HMRC distinguishes it from the general residence certificate, which it does not accept for this claim. The Spanish Tax Agency issues the certificate; we help request it and prepare the claim. Our guide to the Spanish tax residence certificate explains how it is obtained.

In short

  • General rule: taxed only in Spain if you are resident here for treaty purposes
  • Public service pensions: UK taxation, except for Spanish treaty residents who are Spanish nationals; business services follow the general rule
  • Spanish filing duties depend on pension type and annual gross income
  • UK deductions: form DT-Individual Spain
  • AEAT certificate for treaty purposes: Convenio

Frequently asked questions

About your UK pension

Is my UK State Pension taxed in Spain?

Yes, if you are resident in Spain for the purposes of the Spain–UK tax treaty: article 17 assigns the UK State Pension to Spain. This is different from a pension for UK government service, which has its own rule in article 18.2. HMRC form DT-Individual Spain includes a section for the UK State Pension; we check your residence and pension documents before preparing the Spanish return or a claim for UK tax relief.

What if my pension is a UK public service pension?

Pensions paid by the UK State, its political subdivisions or local authorities, either directly or out of funds created by them, for services rendered to them are taxable only in the UK, unless you are resident in Spain for the purposes of the treaty and a Spanish national (article 18.2 of the convention). Pensions for services rendered in connection with a business carried on by the State, its political subdivisions or local authorities follow the general rule (article 18.3). Where they are taxed only in the UK, Spain may take them into account when calculating the tax on the rest of your income (article 22.1.b).

Do I have to file a Spanish income tax return?

Under the law in force on 5 November 2026, a return is required if your annual gross employment income, including a pension treated as employment income, exceeds 15,876 euros and the payer is not required to withhold Spanish income tax (article 96.3.c of the Personal Income Tax Act). A pension exempt in Spain does not count towards that threshold, according to the Spanish Tax Agency. Other income or circumstances can require a return even below it; we check the relevant tax year and your full position.

How do I stop the UK deducting tax?

Apply to HMRC using form DT-Individual Spain with the original AEAT certificate for treaty purposes, Residencia Fiscal en España Convenio. HMRC does not accept the general residence certificate for this treaty claim. The form can also be used to request repayment of UK tax deducted where the treaty gives Spain the taxing right.

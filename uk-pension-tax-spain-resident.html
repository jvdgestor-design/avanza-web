UK pension and living in Spain: where it is taxed and how it is declared

Esta guía en español →

If you are tax resident in Spain and receive a pension from the United Kingdom, the tax treaty between the two countries decides which of them taxes it, and in most cases it is Spain. Grupo Avanza Consultores, tax and accounting adviser in Paterna (Valencia), checks what kind of pension you receive, declares it in your Spanish income tax return and prepares the residence certificate the UK asks for to stop deducting tax. We work remotely across Spain and reply in written English by WhatsApp or email, with a quote before we start.

The general rule: it is taxed in Spain

Pensions and other similar remuneration paid to an individual who is a resident of a State may be taxed only in that State, except for the public pensions covered by article 18.2 (article 17 of the Spain–United Kingdom double taxation convention, in force since 12 June 2014). Under that general rule, if you are resident in Spain, your UK pension is taxed here and not there. The UK itself lists the State Pension, work pensions and purchased annuities on the form a Spanish resident uses to ask for UK tax not to be deducted.

The exception: public service pensions

Pensions paid by the UK State, its political subdivisions or local authorities, either directly or out of funds created by them, for services rendered to them are taxable only in the UK, unless the pensioner is both resident in Spain and a Spanish national, in which case they are taxable only in Spain (article 18.2 of the convention). Someone who also holds Spanish nationality is a Spanish national for this purpose (article 3.1.j). Those paid for services rendered in connection with a business carried on by the State follow the general rule (article 18.3). Where a government pension is taxed only in the UK, Spain may take it into account when calculating the tax on the rest of your income (article 22.1.b).

How it is declared in Spain

Pensions from public social security schemes and other public retirement benefits are declared as employment income (article 17.2.a of the Spanish Personal Income Tax Act). For private pensions, the classification depends on the type of scheme and is checked against its documents; annuities purchased from an insurer, for example, are generally investment income, unless they must be taxed as employment income (articles 17.2.a and 25.3). Because the UK payer is not obliged to withhold Spanish tax, according to the Spanish Tax Agency’s leaflet, the employment income threshold that triggers the duty to file falls from 22,000 to 15,876 euros (article 96.2.a and 3.c); a pension exempt in Spain is not taken into account for the duty to file, according to the Spanish Tax Agency’s leaflet, and anyone entitled to the international double taxation deduction must file in any case (article 96.4). That deduction only covers what the UK may tax under the convention (article 80 of the Act and article 22.1.a of the convention); if UK tax was deducted from a pension the convention assigns to Spain, it is not credited in the Spanish return: it is reclaimed from HMRC.

If the UK keeps deducting tax

To stop UK tax being deducted, or to reclaim it, you send HMRC form “DT-Individual Spain” (version HMRC 10/22), together with the Spanish tax residence certificate issued by the Spanish Tax Agency. Our guide to the Spanish tax residence certificate explains how it is obtained.

What we do

We check whether you are resident in Spain in the relevant year, which pensions you receive and who pays them, and whether any of them is a public service pension. We declare the pension in your Spanish return, request the residence certificate and prepare the form for HMRC. If you arrive this year, we look at it together with your tax residence in the year you move.

  • UK deductions: form DT-Individual Spain
  • With the Spanish Tax Agency residence certificate

Frequently asked questions

About your UK pension

Is my State Pension taxed in Spain or in the UK?

If you are tax resident in Spain, in Spain: pensions paid to a resident of a State may be taxed only in that State (article 17 of the Spain–UK convention). HMRC includes the State Pension on form DT-Individual Spain.

What if my pension is a UK public service pension?

Pensions paid by the UK State, its political subdivisions or local authorities, either directly or out of funds created by them, for services rendered to them are taxable only in the UK, unless you are resident in Spain and a Spanish national (article 18.2 of the convention). Where they are taxed only in the UK, Spain may take them into account when calculating the tax on the rest of your income (article 22.1.b).

Do I have to file a Spanish income tax return?

If your employment income, pension included, exceeds 15,876 euros and the payer is not obliged to withhold Spanish tax, yes (article 96.3.c of the Personal Income Tax Act). A pension exempt in Spain does not count towards that threshold, according to the Spanish Tax Agency’s leaflet. Below that, other circumstances may still require a return, which we check with your details.

How do I stop the UK deducting tax?

By sending HMRC form DT-Individual Spain with the Spanish tax residence certificate issued by the Spanish Tax Agency; the same form is used to reclaim tax already deducted.

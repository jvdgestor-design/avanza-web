An ongoing service

Bookkeeping and tax returns for a business already trading

We record your business invoices, maintain the books or accounts appropriate to your activity and prepare the relevant tax returns. Before filing, we explain the amounts and the information used, and raise any documents or questions still outstanding.

The work can cover:

  • Income and expense records for autónomos, or bookkeeping for small companies.
  • VAT, income tax instalments, withholding returns and company tax, as applicable to your business.
  • Quarterly and annual returns, with the scope set out in your quote.
  • Company year-end accounts and annual accounts where agreed.
  • Questions about invoices, business expenses and the paperwork needed for the next filing.

Send invoices and records digitally through the channel agreed with us. We discuss how you will deliver them and which information we need from you, so you know what to prepare during the year.

If there are missing returns, a letter from the Tax Agency or errors from an earlier period, tell us at the start. We review what needs attention and agree any additional work before taking it on. See also what to prepare when you receive a letter from Hacienda.

When you have employees

Payroll and employment administration

We can handle monthly payroll, Social Security paperwork, employment contracts and employee registrations, changes and departures. We review the contract and applicable collective agreement as part of the work agreed for your employees.

For a quote, tell us how many people you employ, whether you are planning new hires and who currently handles payroll. If an employee is joining or leaving, or a letter has arrived from an authority, include the relevant dates so we can assess the work and timing before accepting it.

Payroll is specified in the quote. We confirm which recurring work and any separate procedures are included.

Moving from your current accountant

Changing tax advisers in Spain

Start with what has already been filed and what is due next. We review your available records, open deadlines and any notice terms in your current agreement before agreeing the handover.

  1. Review the starting position. Tell us your activity, legal form, current adviser and any outstanding filings or notices.
  2. Gather the records. We identify the filed returns and receipts, books or accounting records, current registrations and, if relevant, payroll records needed to continue.
  3. Arrange the handover. With your permission, we request the records from your previous adviser and agree who will handle the next reporting period.
  4. Confirm the work ahead. We agree the scope and quote, and identify any earlier periods that need a separate review.

Tell us about an urgent deadline before changing advisers. The handover needs to make clear who is responsible for the next filing and which records are still missing.

Questions about the service

Can you work with me if my business is outside Valencia?

Yes. We work remotely with autónomos and small companies across Spain from Paterna, Valencia. Before accepting the work, we confirm its scope and whether any step requires your presence.

Can I deal with you in English?

Yes, in writing. You can email us in English and receive written explanations in English. Paperwork for the Spanish authorities is prepared in Spanish.

Are payroll and annual returns included in the same fee?

We specify what is included in your quote before starting. Tell us whether you have employees and which returns or accounts are pending so that the scope covers the work you need.

Can you take over from my current accountant?

We can review the proposed handover, the documents available and any open deadlines. With your permission, we request the records from your previous adviser and agree who will handle the next reporting period.

Tell us about your business

Email your activity, whether you are an autónomo or a company, where you operate, your approximate number of invoices and employees, and whether you already have an adviser. Mention any pending returns or open deadlines.

We will confirm whether we can help and agree the scope and quote before starting. We reply in writing, in English.

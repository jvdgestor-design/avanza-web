Tax on foreign annuities in Spain

If you are tax resident in Spain and bought an annuity from an insurer abroad with your own savings, part of each payment usually goes in the savings section of your Spanish income tax return. The contract tells you which rules apply. A workplace pension or a public retirement benefit follows different rules.

What to declare on your Spanish tax return

This guide covers the ordinary Spanish income tax rules for residents in Spain’s common tax territory. Those rules cover worldwide income. Check the policy and how it was funded. A personal insurance annuity is generally investment income under article 25.3 of the Spanish Income Tax Act. Public retirement benefits and pensions covered by article 17.2 are generally declared as employment income when Spain taxes them. Even a policy issued by an insurer can be part of a workplace pension.

For an insurance annuity taxable in Spain as investment income, use the life-insurance entry in the savings section of the resident return (Modelo 100). Work from the gross payments. If foreign tax was withheld, check whether the applicable treaty allows that country to tax the payment and whether you can claim a Spanish credit for tax actually paid. The credit is subject to limits under article 80.

How much of an immediate annuity is taxable?

For an immediate lifetime annuity, the percentage depends on your age when the annuity was set up and stays the same throughout. For an immediate fixed-term annuity, the percentage depends on the original term. It is 12% for up to 5 years, 16% for over 5 and up to 10 years, 20% for over 10 and up to 15 years, and 25% for over 15 years. See the Spanish Tax Agency’s tables for immediate annuities.

Example for the 2026 tax year

You are 68 when you take out an immediate lifetime annuity on your own behalf with personal savings. Assume you are Spanish tax resident, Spain can tax it and no foreign tax was withheld. You receive 12 gross payments of €500. At age 68, 20% of each payment is taxable investment income. That gives €100 a month, or €1,200 of the €6,000 received. The 20% determines the amount that goes into your savings income. The savings tax rates are applied afterwards.

Contracts that need a different calculation

  • For a deferred annuity, the investment growth before the annuity starts generally also counts. For a lifetime annuity, it is spread over the first 10 years of payments. For a fixed-term annuity, it is spread over the term, up to 10 years.
  • Some retirement or disability insurance payments qualify for a separate calculation. Annuities set up before 2007 may also fall under transitional rules.
  • An inherited right to an annuity is subject to inheritance tax and falls outside the insurance income tax calculations above. If you inherit money and use it to buy your own annuity, apply the income tax rules for the new policy. For a gift of the right to an annuity, check gift tax on the acquisition and the treatment of subsequent payments separately.

What to ask the insurer for

  • The policy and any amendments, showing who paid the premiums, who is entitled to the payments and whether the policy is linked to employment or a pension plan.
  • The date the annuity was set up, your age then and whether the payments are for life or for a fixed term. For a deferred annuity, ask for the premium history and the insurer’s actuarial value at the start of the annuity.
  • A statement for the tax year showing the payer, its country, each payment date, currency and gross amount, and a certificate of any foreign tax withheld or paid.

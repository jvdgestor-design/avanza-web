Spanish gift tax return service for money from family abroad

Request a quoteWhat to send us

Grupo Avanza Consultores, based in Paterna (Valencia), prepares the Spanish gift tax return for people who are tax resident in Spain and receive money from parents or other relatives abroad. We work remotely, for recipients anywhere in Spain, and reply in writing in English, German or French.

Ideally, we review the gift before the money is transferred. Depending on the applicable regional rules, a public deed and evidence of the source and transfer of the funds may be needed to qualify for tax relief. If the money has already arrived, contact us promptly so we can check the documentation and filing deadline.

For the rules themselves (who pays, regional reliefs and deadlines), see our guide to receiving money from abroad in Spain.

The service

What we do for you

  • Check whether the transfer is a gift or something else, such as your own savings or a loan.
  • Confirm which region's rules apply to you and which reliefs may be available.
  • Review the wording of the gift document and the proof of where the money comes from, before the transfer where possible.
  • Prepare the gift tax return and explain the amount and how it is filed in your region.

We send you the prepared return and supporting figures for your review, together with instructions on how and where to file it. Where the Valencian Tax Agency (Agència Tributària Valenciana) is competent, the return is form 651 (official procedure). Whether we can also file it for you depends on the region; we confirm this when agreeing the scope.

Step by step

How it works

  1. Tell us about the gift. Who is giving the money, your relationship, the amount, the date, where you live now and where you have lived in Spain during the previous five years.
  2. We confirm the scope. We tell you what is needed in your case and agree the work before starting.
  3. We review the documents. The gift document and the proof of origin of the funds.
  4. We prepare the return. You receive the completed return, the supporting figures and instructions for filing it with the relevant tax authority.

Documents

What to send us

  • Your NIE or Spanish tax number (NIF) and where in Spain you live.
  • Who is giving the money and your family relationship.
  • The amount, the date or planned date and how it will be transferred.
  • Any document already signed for the gift and the bank records of the transfer.

If something is missing, write to us anyway: we will tell you what is needed.

Before starting

Questions about the service

Who can prepare my Spanish gift tax return in English?

Grupo Avanza Consultores, based in Paterna (Valencia), prepares the Spanish gift tax return for people who are tax resident in Spain and receive money from family abroad. We work remotely and in writing, in English, German or French.

Should I contact you before or after the money arrives?

Before, if possible. The document for the gift and the proof of where the money comes from can affect the regional reliefs. The filing deadline depends on the applicable territory and generally starts when the gift is legally made, which may not be the same date as the bank transfer. Contact us as early as possible so we can check the deadline and the documentation required.

I don't live in the Valencian Community. Can you still help?

Yes. We work with recipients throughout Spain. For gifts of money, we check which territory’s tax rules apply, taking account of your residence history and any special regional tax rules.

Is all money received from abroad a gift?

No. Moving your own savings, a genuine loan or a salary are different cases. We look at what the transfer really is before preparing anything.

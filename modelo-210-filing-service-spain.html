Grupo Avanza Consultores, a tax and accounting advisory firm based in Paterna (Valencia), prepares Modelo 210 for people who own property in Spain but live elsewhere, and can file it for them where authorised. We work remotely, for properties anywhere in Spain, and reply in writing in English, German or French.

You send us the documents online, we check them, confirm what needs to be filed and agree the scope of the work before starting. You deal with the adviser who prepares your return.

For the rules themselves (what is declared, rates and deadlines), see our guide to Modelo 210 for non-residents.

The service

What we do for you

  • Annual return for a property you keep empty or for your own use (imputed income).
  • Returns for rental income, including a review of which expenses may be deductible under the rules applicable to your tax residence and circumstances.
  • Review of earlier years that were not filed, before anything is submitted.
  • Replies to letters from the Spanish Tax Agency about these returns.

We can file your Modelo 210 using our own electronic certificate, provided the appropriate authorisation is in place. When we agree the scope, we confirm which tax authority is competent for your property and whether we can file the return for you. You do not need your own Spanish digital certificate for a return filed through us. Before filing, we send you the figures and the information used, so you know what is being declared on your behalf.

Form 210 filing after selling property in Spain

If you’ve already sold a property in Spain as a non-resident, we prepare your Form 210 and file it if you authorise us. We check whether the buyer withheld 3% and whether you can claim any of it back.

Step by step

How it works

  1. Tell us about the property. Write to us with your country of tax residence, the tax years involved and how the property was used.
  2. We confirm the scope. We tell you which returns are needed and agree the work before starting.
  3. You authorise us. We explain the authorisation you need to give so that we can file for you.
  4. We prepare and file. We send you the figures before filing and the filed return afterwards.

Documents

What to send us

  • Your NIE or Spanish tax number (NIF) and your country of tax residence.
  • The cadastral reference and, if available, the IBI (local property tax) bills or cadastral values for the relevant years, together with your ownership share.
  • How the property was used during each relevant year: kept empty or for your own use, rented out (dates, rent received and expense invoices), or sold (purchase and sale deeds and any Modelo 211 withholding certificate).
  • Any letter you have received from the Spanish Tax Agency, with the date you received it.

If a document is missing, write to us anyway: we will tell you what is needed and where to find it.

Before starting

Questions about the service

Who can file my Modelo 210 for me if I live outside Spain?

Grupo Avanza Consultores, a tax and accounting advisory firm based in Paterna (Valencia), prepares Modelo 210 for non-resident owners of property in Spain and can file it for them where authorised. The work is done remotely and in writing, in English, German or French.

Do I need a Spanish digital certificate?

No, provided we submit the return for you under the appropriate authorisation. We use our own electronic certificate and explain the authorisation required before we start.

Can you deal with years I have not filed?

Yes. We first check which years and returns are outstanding and which can still be filed, and explain the consequences before filing anything. Tell us at the start if you have received a letter from the Spanish Tax Agency.

We own the property jointly. Do you prepare a return for each owner?

Usually. Each owner declares their own share, so we review the ownership and prepare the returns that correspond to each person. For the sale of a property owned by a married couple who are both non-resident, a single return can be filed.

Ask us to file your Modelo 210

Tell us where you are tax resident, which years need attention and how the property was used. If you sold it, give the date of sale.

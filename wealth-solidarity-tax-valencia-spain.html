Start with tax residence and the assets within scope

Your nationality or having a Spanish NIE does not, by itself, determine which assets are taxed. Under the general rules, Spanish tax residents are subject to Wealth Tax on their worldwide wealth, known as obligación personal. Non-residents are taxed on assets and rights located, exercisable or enforceable in Spain, known as obligación real, subject to any restrictions in the applicable tax treaty.

We check your tax residence for the year, the relevant autonomous community, ownership of each asset and any special tax regime that could change the assessment. If you use the impatriate tax regime, a separate review is needed; conclusions under the general residence rules cannot simply be carried across.

The Valencian Wealth Tax allowance for 2026

For taxpayers habitually resident in the Comunitat Valenciana and taxed on their worldwide wealth, the tax-free allowance is €2,000,000. This wording of Article 8 of Valencian Law 13/1997 has applied since 11 August 2026, following Law 5/2026.

For a non-resident, the allowance is the state one of €700,000 (article 28.Tres of Law 19/1991). The fourth additional provision of that law lets non-residents apply the rules of the autonomous community containing the greatest value of their Spanish assets and rights subject to Wealth Tax. However, article 8 of the Valencian law reserves its €2,000,000 allowance for taxpayers with worldwide liability who are habitually resident in the Comunitat Valenciana. If the greatest value is in Valencia, we check all relevant Spanish assets and apply the regional rules consistently, including the tax scale and any applicable reliefs; owning one property in Valencia is not enough to settle the question.

You may need to file Form 714 even when no Wealth Tax is payable

A Wealth Tax return is required if tax remains payable after the applicable deductions and reliefs. It is also required when the tax value of your assets and rights is more than €2,000,000, even if no tax is payable.

For this second test, exempt assets are included and debts, charges and encumbrances are not deducted. The assets considered are those within your worldwide or Spanish-only tax liability, as applicable. This filing threshold and the Valencian allowance currently use the same number, but they are calculated differently and serve different purposes.

Solidarity Tax is a separate calculation: Form 718

Spain’s Temporary Solidarity Tax on Large Fortunes remains in force following its statutory extension. Its taxable event concerns net wealth exceeding €3,000,000, but passing that amount does not automatically mean tax is payable.

It has a €700,000 tax-free allowance, including for non-residents, and the first €3,000,000 of the taxable amount after that allowance is charged at 0%. Wealth Tax actually paid for the year is then deductible, and any applicable tax liability limits and other deductions must be checked. The Valencian Wealth Tax allowance does not replace the state allowance for Solidarity Tax.

Within the scope of this guide, Form 718 is required when the final tax liability is positive. It does not use Form 714’s separate gross-asset filing test. We coordinate both calculations before concluding what is payable and which returns are required.

Homes, investments and debts: evidence matters

The Wealth Tax exemption for a qualifying main home is capped at €300,000 per taxpayer. A second home or holiday property in Spain is not exempt simply because it is your only Spanish property. The Wealth Tax exemptions also apply to Solidarity Tax under the terms of its legislation.

We review the tax values of property, accounts, investments and shareholdings, your ownership share and any exemptions that can be supported. Debts need evidence: for non-residents, deduction is restricted to debts connected with the taxable Spanish assets on the terms set by law. A mortgage secured on a property does not, by itself, establish that the entire debt is deductible.

Do you need a tax representative in Spain?

The representation rules differ between the two taxes. For Wealth Tax, a non-resident must appoint a representative resident in Spain when operating through a permanent establishment or when the tax authority requires it because of the amount and characteristics of the Spanish wealth. Being a non-resident alone does not trigger this requirement.

For Solidarity Tax, non-residents outside the European Union must appoint a representative resident in Spain before the filing deadline. There is an exception for non-EU European Economic Area states covered by rules on mutual assistance in both exchange of information and collection. We also check whether a move before filing creates an additional representation obligation. A legally required tax representative and the authority for our firm to file a return are separate matters.

The 2026 tax point and our work

Both taxes are assessed on 31 December 2026. This guide reflects legislation checked on 4 November 2026; changes adopted before year-end and the filing calendar for each form in 2027, including payment by direct debit, must be checked before filing. The tax assessment date is different from the filing deadline.

For an initial enquiry, tell us the tax year, your country of tax residence, where your assets are located and whether you have filed Wealth Tax or Solidarity Tax before. We then agree the scope, quote and documents needed to review values, debts, exemptions and previous returns. Preparation and filing take place with the appropriate authorisation.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about Spanish Wealth and Solidarity Tax

Must I file Wealth Tax if there is nothing to pay?

Yes, if the tax value of your assets and rights exceeds €2,000,000 within the scope of your worldwide or Spanish-only tax liability. This test includes exempt assets and does not deduct debts, charges or encumbrances. A return is also required when the final tax liability is positive.

Can a non-resident use Valencia’s €2,000,000 allowance?

Valencian law does not provide it for non-residents: its article 8 reserves the €2,000,000 allowance for taxpayers with worldwide liability who are habitually resident in the Comunitat Valenciana, and Law 19/1991 sets the €700,000 state allowance for non-residents (article 28.Tres). If the Comunitat Valenciana contains the greatest value of your Spanish assets and rights, you can apply Valencian rules (fourth additional provision); how that fits with the allowance is something we check case by case. Nationality does not determine this right.

Does wealth above €3,000,000 automatically mean Solidarity Tax is due?

No. There is a €700,000 allowance, including for non-residents, and the first €3,000,000 of the taxable amount after the allowance is charged at 0%. Wealth Tax actually paid and any other applicable deductions are taken into account, as are the tax liability limits. Form 718 is required when the final tax liability is positive.

Is my holiday home exempt up to €300,000?

Not simply because it is a home. The exemption of up to €300,000 per taxpayer is for a main home that meets the legal requirements. A second home or holiday property does not automatically qualify.

Does every non-resident need a tax representative?

The rules differ. Wealth Tax requires one when operating through a permanent establishment or when the tax authority requires it because of the amount and characteristics of the Spanish wealth. Solidarity Tax requires one for non-residents outside the EU, except qualifying EEA states with mutual assistance rules covering information and collection. Other residence changes before filing also need review.

Moving to Spain, living abroad with matters to resolve here, or starting a business? Tell us your situation. We help you identify the procedure and the documents to review before you start.

Our practice is based in Paterna, Valencia. We work remotely with clients across Spain and abroad. Visits to the practice are by appointment.

English support is in writing, by WhatsApp or email. We also provide support in Spanish and in Russian with Anna.

Residence, identity and nationality paperwork

We review the procedure, the documents you have and the status of any existing application. Before accepting the work, we confirm which steps we can handle, any authorisation needed to act for you, and which appointments you will need to attend yourself. Filing on your behalf is included only after we verify a form of representation accepted for the procedure; otherwise, we prepare the documents for you to submit personally.

  • NIE (foreigner identification number)
  • EU citizen registration certificate
  • TIE (foreigner identity card)
  • Arraigo residence routes: segunda oportunidad (second chance), sociolaboral (social and employment), social, socioformativo (education and training) and familiar (family)
  • Family reunification
  • Residence and work permit renewals
  • Long-term residence
  • Spanish nationality by residence

Employment and Social Security

Registration, ongoing employment administration and the paperwork when an employment relationship ends.

  • Self-employed registration and deregistration
  • Monthly payroll and Social Security contributions
  • Employment contracts, extensions and changes
  • Dismissal letters and final settlements
  • Benefits, temporary incapacity and cessation of self-employed activity
  • Assistance with Labour Inspectorate matters

Business and taxes

Tell us the activity you plan to carry out, where you will work and whether you will keep a business in another country.

  • Company formation and dissolution
  • Tax registration and changes (Modelo 036)
  • VAT (IVA), personal income tax (IRPF) and corporate income tax
  • Bookkeeping and filing annual accounts
  • Tax Agency notices, appeals and economic-administrative claims before the competent tribunal
  • Regional and local grants and assistance

Property, inheritance and business premises

Review the tax side before signing a deed, accepting a gift or completing a property transaction.

  • Inheritances and gifts, including cases involving people living abroad
  • Municipal plusvalía tax
  • Buying and selling property and transfer tax (ITP)
  • Business activity licences and changes of licence holder
  • Non-resident tax returns (Modelo 210)

What to tell us in your first message

Describe the procedure, your country of residence, the Spanish city involved and any deadline you have been given. If an application is already in progress, tell us its current stage. A description is enough to start; we then explain which documents we need.

For ongoing tax advice and accounting, see our tax adviser service for foreigners and non-residents.

Before you get started

Can you help if I live outside Spain?

We can review your situation and work remotely. Before accepting the work, we confirm which steps we can handle and whether any appointment requires your presence.

Can I deal with you in English?

Yes, in writing by email or WhatsApp.

What do you need to assess my paperwork?

A description of the situation, your country of residence, the city involved and any deadline you have been given. We then explain which documents we need to review.

Tell us which procedure you need help with

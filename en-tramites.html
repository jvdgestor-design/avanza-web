Tax and your residence situation

An NIE, a TIE, an EU registration certificate, a residence permit or Spanish nationality do not decide where you pay tax. Tax residence is set by article 9 of the Spanish Personal Income Tax Act: more than 183 days in Spain in the calendar year, or the main centre or base of your activities or economic interests here. Does an NIE or a TIE make me tax resident in Spain?

No. Tax residence is decided by article 9 of the Spanish Personal Income Tax Act (Ley del IRPF): you are resident if you spend more than 183 days of the calendar year in Spain, or if the main centre or base of your activities or economic interests is here. Residence is also presumed, unless proven otherwise, if your spouse (not legally separated) and your dependent minor children habitually live in Spain.

Does an NIE or a TIE make me tax resident in Spain?

No. Neither an NIE nor a TIE determines tax residence by itself. This requires an assessment of the criteria in article 9 of Spain’s Personal Income Tax Act: time spent in Spain, the main centre of activities or economic interests and a rebuttable family presumption, with their counting rules and exceptions. Any applicable international tax treaty must also be checked, as required by article 5. We review your circumstances and documents before reaching a conclusion.
